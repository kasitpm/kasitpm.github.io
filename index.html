Background

I'm from Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, and moved to Bengaluru for my degree — a BCA in Analytics at Kristu Jayanti (Deemed to be University), where I'm in my final year. I got into this field because I liked the moment a messy spreadsheet turns into a chart someone can actually decide something from. That's mostly what I still do: clean data, build the model behind it, and put it in front of people in a dashboard they'll open more than once. Outside analytics, I've also worked on embedded systems — my research on adaptive vehicle lighting came out of that side interest. I also volunteer with the National Service Scheme and have served as a student coordinator for Datathone 2025.
